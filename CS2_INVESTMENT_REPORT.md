## Market Context

The current CS2 economy is mainly driven by four forces:

- **Active weekly supply**: community tracking currently points to weekly container drops around Dead Hand Terminal, Genesis Terminal, Kilowatt, Revolution, and Dreams & Nightmares. Active containers are liquid, but supply growth caps upside.
- **Discontinued/legacy scarcity**: older cases and operation rewards have the cleanest long-term thesis because new natural supply is limited or zero.
- **Armory oversupply risk**: Gallery/Fever and many 2024-2026 Armory items have strong demand, but supply is huge. They are often better trades than long-term holds unless bought very cheaply.
- **Weapon demand matters**: AK-47, AWP, M4A1-S/M4A4, Desert Eagle, Glock and USP-S generally retain demand better than SMG/shotgun/pistol filler skins.

## Portfolio Diagnosis

Your portfolio is broad but not equally strong. The best part is the exposure to older cases, operation agents, and a few high-demand weapon playskins. The weakest part is the large amount of cheap modern filler skins and recent/active supply containers. Those can go up during a bull market, but they are likely to underperform scarcer items.

Main overexposures:

- Too many **cheap low-tier skins** from modern cases/collections.
- Too much **active or recent container supply**: Kilowatt, Revolution, Recoil, Fever, Gallery.
- Too many **2024 Major stickers/capsules**, where supply is usually huge.
- Several positions are **liquidity plays**, not scarcity plays.
